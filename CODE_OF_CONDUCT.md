# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Deckory is committed to a respectful, inclusive and constructive community. Contributors, maintainers and users are expected to participate professionally and in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give actionable, technically grounded feedback.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact clearly.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy, attribution and intellectual property.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, deliberate disruption, sexualized conduct and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests or contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or severe violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile contact options. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and handled as promptly and fairly as possible.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
